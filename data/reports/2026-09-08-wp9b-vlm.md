### Results

- glm_ocr: CER 0.002 (pre-1951) and 0.006 (1951 to 2002) on the scanned laws against the GPO text, with the clip anchors found on every granule; 0.029 on the digital era. Its output differs from the PDF text layer of the same page (`STATUTE-10-Pg840-3`: layer `Cha p. XXXIII. — An Actfor the Reliefof`, model `CHAP. XXXIII. — An Act for the Relief of`), so the score is the model's, not the embedded text. The Tesseract baseline of 2026-09-08 (`2026-09-08-wp8-baseline.md`) scored the same eras at 0.181 and 0.252 over the full sample.
- granite_docling: 0.787 mean on pre-1951 is two granules. `STATUTE-10-Pg764` (CER 3.248): the page carries four chapters, the tail anchor of the reference was not found, and the score covers the whole page. `STATUTE-85-Pg844-2` (0.817, kept 0.09) is a repetition loop: the model repeated `John Borbridge, Jr` 293 times and the builder collapsed the repeats. `STATUTE-10-Pg826-5` (0.570, clip both) is character errors on the 1854 print (`A r p r o v e d , August 5, 1854.`; the name `Leach` does not occur in the output).
- lightonocr: 0.022 on pre-1951; the 1951 to 2002 mean of 0.242 is `STATUTE-72-Pg1751` (0.720) and `STATUTE-72-PgA13` (0.397). On both, the builder ended the main body at the end of page 1 and put the remaining pages into `appendix[@role=trailingMatter]` (7,301 of 10,348 characters for Pg1751); the text is in the XML and outside the scored body. Markdown output has no furniture layer, so running heads such as `72 STAT.] PUBLIC LAW 85-910—SEPT. 2, 1958 1753` and marginal dates such as `September 2, 1958 [H.R. 11009]` arrive as body text at the start of each page, where the builder's document-boundary rules see them. `STATUTE-124-Pg2256` (0.169, clip head) is the same pattern on a digital page.
- Section recall on the scanned laws: granite_docling 0.28 and glm_ocr 0.48 on 1951 to 2002, 0.00 for every variant on pre-1951 (the reference has one section per law; no variant's output produced a section element there). Identifier F1 is 0 for every profile, as for the baseline (`pipeline.uslm` assigns no identifiers yet).
- Tier A equals tier B for granite_docling and glm_ocr on the three born-digital laws (the raster and the original page produce the same text). The tier A rasters have a page size of 1912 x 2476 pt (`benchmark/rasterize.py` wraps the 300 dpi PNG without its resolution, so img2pdf uses 96 dpi), and Docling renders them at scale 2 to 3825 x 4951 px; glm_ocr, which has no `max_size`, took 30.8 s/page on them against 9.6 s/page on the 612 x 792 pt originals.

### Geometry and text retention

- granite_docling returns DocTags with bounding boxes (`coord_origin` TOPLEFT), converted to BOTTOMLEFT before the JSON is written. glm_ocr and lightonocr return markdown; Docling assigns every item a zero box, and `pipeline.vlm.finalize_document` gives each item a full-column box stacked in reading order (counted as synthesized boxes above). Sidenote separation by geometry does not apply to them.
- Every text item the models returned is in the JSON; `kept` is 1.00 to 1.05 for every row except the granite_docling repetition loop (0.09).
